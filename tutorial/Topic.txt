question hai. Tumhe confusion hona normal hai, kyunki LangChain aur LangGraph ke concepts overlap karte hain.
Simple answer:

Project mein hum LangChain + LangGraph dono use karenge.
Dono ek dusre ke replacement nahi hain.

Socho:

LangChain = individual AI components/tools
LangGraph = un components ko workflow/agent system mein connect karna
